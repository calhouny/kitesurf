Quarter-Sphere Wind Window

Wind blows downwind ahead of the rider (-Z) with your kite’s inflatable leading-edge bladder oriented upwind (+Z, behind you). Where you park or dive the kite controls speed, tack direction, boost power, and airborne swing.

12 O’Clock Zenith (75°–88° Elevation)

Neutral overhead lull slows forward drive to an idle drift. Edge hard, build tension (LOAD & POP!), and tap SPACE to boost vertically into Big Air.

Port & Starboard Sweet-Spot Pockets (35°–40°)

Steer left or right and release keys: in default Free Ride, the kite parks around ~38° elevation and drives clean 80°–90° crosswind tacks.

Center Power Zone & Kiteloop Arc

Sweeping the kite down through 18°–55° builds explosive apparent wind. Triggering a mid-air Kiteloop whips a wide ±38° circular sweep through the power zone.

Upwind Leading-Edge Bladder Orientation

The inflatable leading-edge tube faces upwind (+Z, behind you) at 12 o’clock, tilts toward the water when diving, and rotates smoothly back upright when raised.

Pro Techniques & Real Wind Physics

Every knot of speed and meter of air comes straight from the wind window. Learn these five fundamentals to dominate heats.

12 O’Clock Overhead Lull

Parking the kite overhead (75°–88° near center) puts it in the neutral zenith lull—forward drive drops near zero and water drag slows you to a relaxed drift.

  • Use 12 o’clock to pause, line up a kicker ramp, or transition smoothly from a left tack to a right tack.
  • Drop the kite below 75° into the side pocket or dive through the power zone to accelerate back past 30+ knots.

Free Ride Crosswind (Default) vs Course Pull

Free Ride is active by default: steering sideways arcs the kite into the 35°–40° parking pocket where releasing keys holds it steady (~38°) while driving 80°–90° crosswind tacks.

  • To switch tacks, steer inward / press W to climb over 12 o’clock, then dive down the opposite side.
  • Toggle FREE RIDE ↔ COURSE PULL anytime from the Title Screen, Top HUD, or Pause Menu if you want gentle centering assist down the corridor.

Big Air Boosts (Load & Pop!)

Maximum jump height comes from converting horizontal board speed + line tension into vertical lift at 12 o’clock.

  • Dive the kite on a fast tack until the Line Tension bar illuminates LOAD & POP!
  • Send the kite swiftly up toward 12 o’clock, sheet in with W, and hit SPACE (or launch off a wave crest / Kicker Ramp) for 15m–40m+ Big Air.

Wide Sweeping Kiteloops & Airborne Pendulum

Pressing SPACE (or SHIFT/E) in mid-air kicks off a wide ~1.38s loop that dives the kite through the power zone (±38° sweep) for a brief horizontal yank and a gentle late catch.

  • Repeated loops in one jump decay quickly in pull—loops enhance style and distance without infinite climbing.
  • Airborne Pendulum: Leaving the kite off-center in the air lets gravity swing you underneath the kite fulcrum while the kite drifts back toward overhead support.

Trick Combos, Multipliers & Clean Landings

Stack rotations (J/L), grabs (I/K), wave-lip boosts, and mid-air kiteloops in one flight to climb up to a 10x multiplier (with a 4.2s combo decay window on water). Release rotations before touchdown so the twin-tip aligns cleanly for a Clean / Steezy / Mega Boost Landing speed surge—and never loop too close to the water or slam sideways, or a wipeout cancels unbanked combo points!
